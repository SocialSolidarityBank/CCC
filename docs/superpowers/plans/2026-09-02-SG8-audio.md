## Global Constraints

- Community Cloud original audio is stored only in institution-owned Supabase private Storage.
- Upload targets are fixed at two hours and use `upsert: false`; Agent download targets are minted on demand for a live claim and remain valid for at most 600 seconds.
- Direct Supabase signed GET bearer URLs cannot be revoked after mint; residual lifetime is bounded to 600 seconds, never logged, and never re-minted after lease expiry, release, or consent withdrawal.
- People and browsers never receive a signed GET or human audio GET; public and long-lived URLs are prohibited.
- `AudioStore` accepts a `ReadableStream<Uint8Array>`, streams with backpressure, and rejects every body above 209,715,200 bytes.
- The only accepted canonical MIME values are `audio/mp4`, `audio/mpeg`, `audio/wav`, `audio/x-wav`, `audio/webm`, and `audio/x-m4a`.
- The key grammar is exactly `audio/<sessionId>/<uuid>` with traversal and alternate prefixes rejected.
- The retention clock starts only after the next-business-day gate and an object-specific successful claim by an eligible Agent with configured, healthy provider, capacity, and route-specific consent; `local` requires recording consent only, while `azure`/external requires recording plus external-STT consent. Global poll, failed claim, STT off, provider-unhealthy, and stale/withdrawn receipt events do not start it.
- The 24-hour processing deadline starts at that real opportunity as `min(opportunity_at + 24h, retention_hard_cap_at)`. Upload plus 24 hours is never the deletion clock; without a real opportunity no processing deadline is invented.
- Upload admission itself requires recording consent plus route-specific consent and a currently healthy eligible Agent with capacity; if it fails, original audio is rejected and the manual-note path is used.
- `retention_hard_cap_at` is `uploaded_at + 7*24h` absolute UTC. At the cap, original audio is deleted regardless of opportunity or result, with administrator incident and manual-note outbox.
- Business days use versioned `kr-business-days-v1.json` in `Asia/Seoul`; weekends, Korean public/substitute holidays, and calendar-declared organization closures are non-business days. Missing/stale calendar fails closed for new target issuance and date calculation.
- D8 requires an administrator alert after six hours without a successful queue poll and a daily repeat until resolved; it requires manual-note fallback, no provider auto-fallback, and no re-record request.
- Successful processing means durable STT/result commit plus processed deletion intent in one DB batch/outbox boundary, followed by immediate delete/unlink on every adapter; input/hash verification alone never schedules processed deletion.
- Every terminal deletion state requires fresh `deleteSucceeded`, `absentFromList`, `absentFromMetadata`, and `directReadAbsent` booleans, bound to generation and attempt. Cloud re-deletes/re-verifies after upload capability expiry plus 60 seconds; each delete is followed by its adapter propagation wait before fresh verification.
- Local Single, Local Office, and the R2 reference adapter return `null` for both target methods and expose streamed API bodies.
- Expiry, consent withdrawal, incident, and manual-note obligations are one idempotent DB batch/outbox; reconcile runs every five minutes with exponential backoff capped at one hour and alerts persistent failures.
- `확정` means contract, rules, mode differences, fixtures, and verification commands are complete; it does not require adapters or runtime evidence.
